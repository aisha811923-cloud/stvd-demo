## 5. Historical Milestones & Three Decades of Growth

- **1996 — Inception & Foundation:**  
  The Prabhudasi Sisters of Ajmer consecrated St. Teresa Vidya Deep on Saveena-Jogi Talaab Road to bring values-based English-medium schooling to southern Udaipur. Initial intake commenced with foundational primary scholars under the motto *"SEEK YE WISDOM"*.
- **2005 — CBSE Affiliation & Secondary Wing (AISSE):**  
  Achieved formal affiliation with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi. Inaugurated the Class X All India Secondary School Examination (AISSE) center with the maiden board batch achieving a **100% pass distinction**. Built dedicated composite science and computing laboratories.
- **2014 — Senior Secondary Upgradation (AISSCE Tri-Stream):**  
  CBSE granted senior secondary status for Classes XI and XII. Expanded state-of-the-art Physics, Chemistry, and Biology laboratories and launched dedicated departments for Science, Commerce, and Humanities. Graduates began qualifying for national competitive examinations (NEET, JEE, CUET, CA Foundation).
- **2026–Present — Campus Modernization & Digital Horizon:**  
  Celebrating 30 years of educational service. Installed interactive smart classroom displays, established high-speed digital IT coding suites with fiber connectivity, introduced an automated admissions desk, and reinforced safety transit fleets across Udaipur.

## 6. Academic Architecture & Curriculum Wings (NEP 2020 Aligned)

The academic structure is organized into five progressive developmental stages adhering to the **5+3+3+4 structure** of the National Education Policy (NEP 2020):

### 1. Foundational Wing (Nursery, LKG, UKG)
- **Target Age:** 3 to 5 Years
- **Pedagogical Approach:** Play-Way & Sensory Discovery; activity-based learning without premature academic stress.
- **Core Curriculum:** Phonics & Early Reading, Number Play & Spatial Reasoning, Expressive Arts & Music, Gross & Fine Motor Development.
- **Key Features:** Montessori-aligned learning corners, child-safe activity zones, emotional transition support, bilingual English & Hindi vocabulary development.
- **Special Schedule Note:** Nursery classes observe complete holidays on all Saturdays.

### 2. Primary Wing (Classes I to V)
- **Target Age:** 6 to 10 Years
- **Pedagogical Approach:** Experiential & Conceptual Building; transitioning into structured literacy and numeracy.
- **Core Curriculum:** English Language & Literature, Mathematics & Mental Math, Environmental Studies (EVS), Hindi Language, Foundational Computer Literacy.
- **Key Features:** Interactive smart board demonstrations, experiential science kits, weekly moral value story hours, public speaking exercises, guided junior library reading.

### 3. Middle Wing (Classes VI to VIII)
- **Target Age:** 11 to 13 Years
- **Pedagogical Approach:** Inquiry-Driven & Analytical Thinking; fostering logical questioning and investigative problem-solving.
- **Core Curriculum:** Integrated Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), Mathematics & Geometry, Social Sciences (History, Civics, Geography), Languages (English, Hindi, Sanskrit), Information & Communication Technology (ICT).
- **Key Features:** Hands-on practical lab sessions, inter-house debates, literary clubs, intra-school sports leagues, yoga and physical fitness drills, diagnostic monthly unit tests.

### 4. Secondary Wing (Classes IX & X)
- **Target Age:** 14 to 15 Years
- **Pedagogical Approach:** CBSE All India Secondary School Examination (AISSE) Board Preparation.
- **Core Curriculum:** Science (with laboratory practical viva assessments), Mathematics (Standard / Basic options), Social Science, English Language & Literature, Hindi Course-A / Sanskrit, Information Technology (Vocational).
- **Key Features:** Pre-board mock examinations mirroring official CBSE marking schemes, regular laboratory practical evaluations, personalized doubt-clearing clinics, career and stream orientation for Senior Secondary selection.

### 5. Senior Secondary Wing (Classes XI & XII — Tri-Stream Specialization)
- **Target Age:** 16 to 17 Years
- **Pedagogical Approach:** High-Rigor Tri-Stream Specialization for CBSE AISSCE Board distinctions and national competitive entrance examinations.
- **Streams & Subject Combinations:**
  1. **Science Stream (Medical & Non-Medical):**
     - Physics, Chemistry, Biology / Mathematics, Physical Education / Computer Science, English Core.
     - *Focus:* Extensive laboratory experimentations, problem-solving speed, NEET & JEE orientation.
  2. **Commerce Stream:**
     - Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, Applied Mathematics / Informatics Practices, English Core.
     - *Focus:* Financial principles, corporate case analysis, ledger accounting, CA Foundation and CUET readiness.
  3. **Humanities / Arts Stream:**
     - History, Political Science, Economics / Geography, Psychology / Sociology, English Core.
     - *Focus:* Critical social analysis, essay writing mastery, public administration, civil services orientation.

## 7. Assessment & Evaluation Framework

The school follows CBSE's Continuous and Comprehensive Assessment strategy:

1. **Diagnostic Monthly Unit Tests:** Formative cycles conducted each month to identify conceptual learning gaps early and provide timely remedial coaching before term examinations.
2. **Terminal Summative Examinations:**
   - **Term 1 (Half-Yearly Examination):** Conducted in September.
   - **Term 2 (Annual Examination):** Conducted in March.
   - Both follow strict CBSE marking criteria, question design, and grading scales.
3. **Board Examination Preparedness (Classes X & XII):** Full-syllabus pre-board mock examination series, previous-year question bank analysis, and dedicated laboratory viva simulations.
4. **Mandatory Second Saturday Parent-Teacher Conclaves (PTM):** Monthly structured conferences where parents receive individual student report cards and discuss academic progress, attendance records, and behavioral development directly with subject teachers.
