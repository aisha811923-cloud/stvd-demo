## 4. Academic Architecture
* **Academic Cycle:** April to March (Two distinct semesters divided by winter break).
* **Class Divisions:**
  * **Foundational / Pre-Primary Wing:** Nursery, LKG, UKG
  * **Primary Wing:** Classes I to V
  * **Middle Wing:** Classes VI to VIII
  * **Secondary Wing:** Classes IX & X
  * **Senior Secondary Wing (Streams):** Classes XI & XII
    * Science (Medical & Non-Medical: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics)
    * Commerce (Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics)
    * Arts / Humanities
* **Assessment & Evaluation:** Periodic continuous evaluations, unit tests, and terminal reports requiring parent acknowledgment.
* **Parent Engagement:** Mandatory Second Saturday Parent–Teacher Meetings (PTMs).

---

## 5. Campus Infrastructure & Student Life
* **STEM & Academic Facilities:** Well-equipped science laboratories (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), computer and multimedia labs, and a centralized reading library.
* **Sports & Physical Training:** Mandatory physical drill sessions, athletics tracks, playground spaces for volleyball, football, and indoor game arenas.
* **Safety & Security:** CCTV surveillance across campus corridors, boundary monitoring, and school-supervised transportation connectivity across Udaipur routes.
* **Co-Curriculars:** Annual Cultural Days, Sports Meets, Debates, Exhibitions, and National Celebrations (Gandhi Jayanti, Independence Day, Republic Day, Christmas).

## 7. Recommended UI/UX & Design Tokens
* **Color Palette:**
  * **Primary (Prestige Navy):** `#0F1E36` (Deep authority, structural headers)
  * **Secondary (Burgundy / Crimson):** `#6B1D2F` (Academic heritage, badge accents)
  * **Accent (Warm Gold):** `#D4AF37` / `#C59B27` (CTA buttons, achievement borders)
  * **Neutral Background (Parchment White):** `#F8F9FB` (Light, readable backgrounds)
  * **Surface Cards:** `#FFFFFF` with soft shadows (`box-shadow: 0 10px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`)
* **Typography Pairing:**
  * **Headings:** `Playfair Display` or `Cinzel` (Editorial, timeless school crest feel)
  * **Body & UI Elements:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Inter` (Crisp readability on mobile viewports)
* **Motion Directives:**
  * Subtle `y: 20 -> 0`, `opacity: 0 -> 1` stagger triggers on scroll via Framer Motion.
  * Smooth accordion transitions for syllabus and FAQ components.
  * Spring-animated modal drawers for the mobile quick-callback form.
