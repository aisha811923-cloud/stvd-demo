# Institutional Design System: St. Teresa Vidya Deep

## 1. Color Palette
* **Navy 950 (Oxford Midnight):** `#080F1D` (Body text, deep contrasts)
* **Navy 900 (Prestige Deep Blue):** `#0F1E36` (Headers, hero backgrounds)
* **Gold 500 (Imperial Academic Gold):** `#D4AF37` (CTA buttons, borders, highlights)
* **Crimson 800 (Heritage Wine):** `#6B1D2F` (School emblem badges, alerts)
* **Parchment White (Surface Base):** `#F8F9FB` (Light section backgrounds)
* **Pure White (Elevated Surface):** `#FFFFFF` (Bento cards, modal surfaces)

## 2. Typography
* **Serif Display (Hero & Section Titles):** `Cinzel`, `Playfair Display`, serif
* **Clean Sans (Body, Form Elements, Captions):** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif
* **Scale:**
  * Hero Title: `text-4xl md:text-6xl lg:text-7xl font-bold tracking-tight font-serif`
  * Section Headers: `text-2xl md:text-4xl font-semibold font-serif text-navy-900`
  * Body Text: `text-base md:text-lg leading-relaxed text-slate-600`
  * Micro Copy: `text-xs font-medium uppercase tracking-widest text-gold-600`
