# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
**Project:** St. Teresa Vidya Deep Sr. Sec. School Flagship Web Portal
**Institution:** St. Teresa Vidya Deep Senior Secondary School, Udaipur
**Target Audience:** Prospective parents, enrolled students, school administration, CBSE inspection boards

## 1. Executive Summary
Transform the existing static web presence of St. Teresa Vidya Deep into an editorial, high-converting digital experience. The platform must balance institutional heritage (established 1996, managed by Prabhudasi Sisters) with modern pedagogical excellence, featuring a frictionless admissions lead-generation engine and smooth, fluid motion design.

## 2. Core Personas
1. **Prospective Parent (Primary Conversion Target):** Seeks curriculum standards, infrastructure quality, fee/admissions process, and quick callbacks with minimal friction.
2. **Current Parent / Student (Utility User):** Requires instant access to academic calendars, examination notices, event dates, and mandatory CBSE compliance disclosures.
3. **School Administrator / Counselor (Internal Operator):** Needs immediate lead intake via phone/WhatsApp callbacks without losing leads in buried email inboxes.

## 3. Key Feature Specifications
* **High-Conversion Quick Lead Engine:** Persistent floating "Admissions 2026-27 Open" badge launching an un-intimidating modal (Parent Name, WhatsApp/Phone, Student Name, Grade Applying).
* **Institutional Storytelling Bento:** Interactive grid showcasing student-teacher ratio, campus security, laboratory infrastructure, and CBSE excellence metrics.
* **Curriculum Wing Matrix:** Dynamic interactive navigation through Pre-Primary, Primary, Middle, Secondary, and Senior Secondary (Science, Commerce, Arts).
* **Interactive Campus Showcase:** High-resolution virtual facility walk-through with modal zoom for Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Computer labs.
* **Mandatory Disclosure Vault:** Fast-loading, searchable regulatory drawer fulfilling CBSE affiliation requirements.
* **Omni-Device Responsiveness:** Bespoke UX across 360px mobile viewports, 768px/1024px tablets, and ultra-wide desktops.
